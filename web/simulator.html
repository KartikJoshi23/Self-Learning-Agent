Physics runs live in your browser: soiling accumulates from measured aerosol optical depth, rain above 6 mm washes the panel and leaves a 14-day damp grace period, and energy comes from a pvlib chain fitted to within 0.003% of the full model. Soiling is calibrated to DEWA's measured 0.14–0.33 %/day; energy is valued at the MBR Solar Park phase-5 PPA tariff of 1.6953 US¢/kWh, cleaning at $60/MWp per pass.
